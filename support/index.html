Portage Support

Portage is a player and downloader for your own media server — Plex, Emby or Jellyfin — for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Need help or found a bug?
Email hi@rikard.dev. Include what you were doing, the device, and the server type if it matters. Replies usually come within a few days.

Which servers work?

Any Plex, Emby or Jellyfin server you already have access to. Sign in with your Plex account, or add a server by address. Portage has no account of its own and hosts no content.

Where do downloads go?

  • iPhone and iPad: the Files app, under On My iPhone → Portage.
  • Mac: Movies → Portage in your home folder.

Casting

  • Chromecast and Google TV are found automatically on your network.
  • DLNA TVs are added by their IP address from the cast menu.
  • Web browsers: open tv.rikard.dev on the screen you want to watch on; it shows a pairing code. Choose Web browser from the cast menu in the app and enter that code, or scan the QR. On the same network you can also open the local address the app displays.

A file won't play or cast

Some TVs and browsers can't decode every format. Portage offers to convert such files on the device; the conversion runs in the background and casts when it's ready.
