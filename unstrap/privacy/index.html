Unstrap Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026

Unstrap collects no data. Everything it records stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

Raw sensor packets from your wearable and the metrics computed from them: heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep stages, activities, blood oxygen, skin temperature, respiratory rate and derived scores. They are kept in a database inside the app's own storage on the phone.

What leaves the phone

Nothing, unless you choose it. The app has no account, no server and no analytics, and makes no network requests. Two actions move data off the phone, both started by you:

  • Write to Apple Health writes sleep, heart rate, HRV, oxygen saturation and respiratory rate samples to the Health app on the same phone, after you grant access. Apple Health data is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Unstrap does not read Health data for any other purpose.
  • Export Snapshot hands a copy of the database file to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used only to talk to the wearable you pair. The app does not scan for or record other devices.

Notifications

Reminders and sync status are local notifications scheduled on the phone. No push notification service is used.

Deleting your data

Clearing local data in Settings removes everything the app has stored. Deleting the app does the same. Samples already written to Apple Health are removed from the Health app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new effective date. Questions: hi@rikard.dev.
